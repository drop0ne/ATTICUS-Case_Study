# Notice and disclosure boundary

This repository publishes the **experiment, measurements, scoring record, statistical analysis, and post-hoc audit** for the ATTICUS case study.

It does **not** publish the ATTICUS framework itself.

Restricted material includes, without limitation:
- proprietary ATTICUS system/framework instructions;
- internal module definitions and implementation details;
- hidden governance logic;
- request-only architecture material;
- any non-public manuscript or source text that would reconstruct the framework.

The public case study intentionally describes ATTICUS only at the level required to identify the experimental condition and interpret the results.

This restriction creates a reproducibility limitation. Qualified independent reviewers who need controlled access to the ATTICUS condition should contact the repository owner.

The First Proof problems, human solutions, institutional submissions, and upstream repository are the work of their respective authors and organizations. This repository links to the upstream public repository rather than republishing the full upstream archive.
