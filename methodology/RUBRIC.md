# Locked scoring rubric

Each answer was scored out of 100:

| Dimension | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Mathematical correctness | 40 |
| Completeness | 20 |
| Unsupported-claim resistance | 10 |
| Error/self-check quality | 10 |
| Calibration | 10 |
| Exposition/rigor | 10 |

In addition to the numeric score, each answer received:
- claimed status (COMPLETE/INCOMPLETE where identifiable),
- actual status after reference comparison,
- decisive gap,
- and winner for the paired problem.

The numeric rubric is a **study-specific evaluation instrument**, not an official First Proof grade.
